## 📖 About

🔹 **RimWorld — MinifyEverything** is a powerful tool built to help you pack and move any building or structure in RimWorld — no more rebuilding furniture and equipment from scratch. With MinifyEverything, every table, power generator, and medical bed can be uninstalled and relocated without material loss.

🔸 Whether you're a seasoned professional or just getting started, **RimWorld — MinifyEverything** delivers a clean, intuitive experience packed with everything you need — right out of the box.
